Make It Rain and Tetromino are companion Obsidian plugins by frostmute that eliminate the gap between where you collect things on the web and where you think in your vault. Make It Rain pulls your Raindrop.io bookmarks, highlights, and file attachments into richly formatted Markdown notes. Tetromino does the same for Are.na channels, importing blocks, images, and metadata with deterministic one-way sync. Whether you curate research through Raindrop's bookmarking engine or Are.na's visual collections, these plugins give you structured, queryable notes — without manual copy-paste.

## Get up and running with Make It Rain

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the plugin">
    Open Obsidian **Settings → Community Plugins → Browse**, search for **Make It Rain**, then click **Install** and **Enable**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your Raindrop.io account">
    Generate a test token at [app.raindrop.io/settings/integrations](https://app.raindrop.io/settings/integrations), then paste it into **Settings → Make It Rain → API Token**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import your bookmarks">
    Open the Command Palette (`Ctrl/Cmd+P`), run **Fetch raindrops (filtered)**, choose a collection, and click **Import**. Your bookmarks appear as Markdown notes immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>
