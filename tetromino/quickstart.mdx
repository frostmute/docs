---
title: "Get Started with Tetromino: Install and First Import"
sidebarTitle: "Quickstart"
description: "Install Tetromino, connect your Are.na account with a personal access token, and run your first Are.na channel import into Obsidian in minutes."
---

This guide walks you through everything you need to run your first Are.na import: installing the plugin, authenticating with the Are.na API, mapping your channels, and triggering the import. By the end, your Are.na channels will appear as organized Markdown notes inside your vault.

<Steps>

  <Step title="Install Tetromino from Community Plugins">
    Open Obsidian and go to **Settings → Community Plugins**. If restricted mode is on, click **Turn on community plugins**. Search for **Tetromino**, click **Install**, then click **Enable**.

    You can also install manually by downloading `main.js`, `manifest.json`, and `styles.css` from the [latest GitHub release](https://github.com/frostmute/Tetromino/releases/latest) and placing them in `<vault>/.obsidian/plugins/tetromino/`.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Get your Are.na personal access token">
    Tetromino uses the Are.na REST API v3 with Bearer token authentication.

    1. Go to [are.na/developers/personal-access-tokens](https://www.are.na/developers/personal-access-tokens).
    2. Sign in to your Are.na account if prompted.
    3. Create a new personal access token and copy it — you won't be able to see it again after closing the page.

    Keep this token private. Tetromino stores it locally in Obsidian's plugin data and never transmits it outside the Are.na API.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add your token in Tetromino settings">
    In Obsidian, go to **Settings → Community Plugins → Tetromino**. Paste your personal access token into the **API Token** field. Click **Verify** to confirm the token is valid before proceeding.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Import your channel mappings">
    Scroll down to the **Channel management** section in Tetromino settings and click **Import my channels**. Tetromino fetches all channels from your Are.na account and creates a mapping for each one, defaulting to `Are.na/<channel-slug>/` as the vault folder. Each new mapping is enabled automatically.

    You can also add mappings manually by clicking **+ Add mapping** and entering a channel slug — the part of the Are.na URL after `/channel/`.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Run your first import">
    Open the Obsidian command palette with `Ctrl/Cmd+P` and run **Import all channels now**. Tetromino fetches all blocks from every enabled channel, converts them to Markdown notes, and writes them to your vault. A sync summary modal appears when the import finishes.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Explore your vault">
    After the import completes, open the file explorer. Your channels appear under `Are.na/`:

    ```
    Vault/
    └── Are.na/
        ├── overview.md
        ├── my-research-channel/
        │   ├── index.md
        │   ├── Block Title.md
        │   └── Another Block.md
        └── import-history.md
    ```

    - **`overview.md`** — a master note linking to every imported channel
    - **`<channel-slug>/index.md`** — the channel index note listing all its blocks
    - **`<channel-slug>/<Block Title>.md`** — one note per Are.na block
    - **`import-history.md`** — an append-only log of every completed import
  </Step>

</Steps>

<Tip>
  Before your first real import, run **Preview import (dry-run)** from the command palette. Tetromino fetches all channel data and shows you exactly which files would be created or updated — without writing a single file to your vault. It's the safest way to verify your mappings are correct before committing.
</Tip>
