Make It Rain is an Obsidian community plugin that pulls your Raindrop.io bookmarks directly into your vault as structured Markdown notes. This page explains what the plugin does, the problems it solves, the features it offers, and what you need before you install it. If you're ready to jump straight to setup, head to the [Quickstart](/quickstart).

## The problem it solves

Raindrop.io is one of the most capable bookmark managers available — it handles links, articles, images, videos, PDFs, audio, and books, and lets you organize them into collections and groups, tag them, highlight passages, and leave personal notes. Obsidian is where your thinking happens, connecting ideas across Markdown files in a personal knowledge graph.

The two tools don't talk to each other out of the box. If you want your saved articles in your vault, you copy and paste. If you want your highlights next to your own notes, you type them out. Make It Rain closes that gap: it reads your Raindrop account via the official API and writes your bookmarks into your vault as proper Markdown files — with YAML frontmatter, highlight sections, tag links, cover images, and optionally the full article text or downloaded file attachment.

## Quick Import and Highlights Aggregation

Beyond the main bulk import flow, Make It Rain includes two focused commands. **Quick Import** lets you paste a single Raindrop URL or ID and import that one bookmark instantly — useful when you save something and want it in your vault right away. **Aggregate Highlights by Tag** collects all highlighted passages that share a given tag from across your vault and consolidates them into a single summary note, making it easy to review and connect your annotations.

## Requirements

Before you install Make It Rain, make sure you have the following:

- **Obsidian 1.13.0 or later** — the plugin will not load on older versions.
- **A Raindrop.io account** — a free account is sufficient; a Pro account unlocks full-text search in Raindrop but is not required for Make It Rain to work.
- **A Raindrop.io API test token** — generated at [app.raindrop.io/settings/integrations](https://app.raindrop.io/settings/integrations) (no cost, takes about one minute).

Make It Rain works on both desktop and mobile Obsidian. No internet connection is required after initial import — your notes live entirely in your vault.
